Cryptographic Adherence Verification

Every dose confirmation produces a deterministic SHA-256 event digest that can be anchored to Ethereum Sepolia. This cryptographically proves that the recorded timestamp and status have not been altered or backdated. No personal names or prescriptions are ever put on-chain.
